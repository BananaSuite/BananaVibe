# Third-party software

The sandbox installs OpenCode 1.18.18, maintained at
[anomalyco/opencode](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode). It is distributed
under the MIT license; the full notice is in
[sandbox/OPENCODE-LICENSE](sandbox/OPENCODE-LICENSE) and is copied into the image.
The build verifies the published release archive checksum before installing it.

Node.js, Python and Debian packages in the sandbox image retain their respective
licenses and notices. Their installed packages contain those notices. BananaVibe's
AGPL license does not replace the licenses of independent dependencies.
